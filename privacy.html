Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This policy explains how the public Bidova website handles information. It covers the website at bidova.github.io, not the Bidova desktop applications or a future customer account service.

Information you choose to provide

If you use the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, company name (optional), inquiry topic, and message. We use that information to review and respond to your inquiry and handle related support or demo requests. Please do not submit passwords, confidential proposals, financial information, or sensitive project documents.

Contact form provider

Contact form submissions are processed by Formspree, a third-party form service. Your submission is sent to that provider for delivery to Bidova. Formspree's own privacy practices also apply.

Website analytics

The website includes Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate website usage and performance. Cloudflare may process technical request and usage information under its own policies. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Ask Bidova

The website's current Ask Bidova helper uses predefined public information and handbook content in your browser. It is not a live AI service, and its chat questions are not intentionally sent to an AI provider or stored as a server-side conversation by this website. Do not enter confidential information into the helper.

Other services and links

The website may load fonts from Google Fonts and link to other websites. Those services may process information according to their own policies. Following an external link takes you to a site outside Bidova's control.

Retention and sharing

We use inquiry details to respond to requests and may retain correspondence as needed for follow-up, support, and recordkeeping. We share information with service providers needed to operate the website and respond to inquiries, or when required by law. We do not claim to sell contact-form submissions. Specific provider retention periods and deletion schedules have not yet been established in this policy.

Your choices and requests

You can browse without submitting the contact form. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted, use the contact page and identify the email address used in your original message. We will review requests subject to applicable obligations and the limits of third-party services.

Security

We take reasonable care in operating the website, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid sharing sensitive project or account information through the public contact form.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website's features or providers change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.

Questions about privacy?

Contact Bidova using the website contact form.
